# Project Context & Product Philosophy

## Product Vision
The application is a **Gamified Crypto Trading Education Simulator**. Users trade cryptocurrencies using **REAL market prices** but entirely **VIRTUAL money (₹100,000)**.

- **NO real-money trading**
- **NO cryptocurrency purchase**
- **NO deposits or withdrawals**
- **NO crypto-wallet connection**
- **NO exchange-account connection**

## Core Philosophy
> **"Profit does not necessarily mean you made a good decision."**

The platform emphasizes discipline over luck:
- A user who gambles 90% of their equity into an altcoin without a stop-loss may get lucky and profit, but receives a **LOW Discipline Score (e.g. 25/100)** and a **HIGH Risk Score (e.g. 95/100)**.
- A user who executes a disciplined trade with a 5% position size and a stop-loss receives a **HIGH Discipline Score (e.g. 90/100)** regardless of whether the trade hit the stop-loss or profit target.
